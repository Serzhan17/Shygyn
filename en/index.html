The problem

Money slips away in two places — and you rarely see both

Most people track spending in notes apps or not at all, and pick a bank for a loan based on ads rather than actual terms.

01

Small expenses add up to a large sum

Subscriptions, card purchases, recurring payments — unnoticeable one by one, but significant over a month if nobody is counting.

02

Banks for loans get picked at random

The rate difference between banks can cost hundreds of thousands of tenge in overpayment over the loan term — but almost nobody compares terms by hand.

03

Spending data is never connected to loan choices

Money-tracking apps don’t tell you where to borrow cheaper. Loan calculators don’t see your real spending history.

The solution

Who Shygyn is for, and what it gives you

Individuals

The full picture, no spreadsheets

Card purchases are logged automatically and sorted into categories, and cash expenses take just a couple of taps to add — you see where your money goes without opening Excel.

When choosing a loan

The right bank for your goal

Based on your real financial history, Shygyn compares banks and shows where it’s cheaper to get a mortgage, auto loan, or business loan.

For companies

Financial care for your team

A corporate subscription gives employees a personal financial assistant as part of their benefits — no extra load on HR or accounting.
